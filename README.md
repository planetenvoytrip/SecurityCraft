## 📖 What Is SecurityCraft?

**SecurityCraft** is a comprehensive security mod that adds all the defensive tools you need to protect your base. It includes locks, keypads, mine traps, laser tripwires, cameras, retinal scanners, reinforced blocks, and much more.

The mod is designed for **both singleplayer and multiplayer**. In singleplayer you can lock your chests from mobs and wandering players. In multiplayer you can build a fully automated defense grid that identifies friend from foe and responds accordingly.

Everything is highly configurable — from who can break which blocks to how many attempts a keypad allows before locking out. You build the security system that fits your needs.

**Key highlights:**
- ✅ **50+ security blocks and items** — Locks, mines, lasers, cameras, reinforced blocks
- ✅ **Customizable keypads and passwords** — Set your own codes
- ✅ **Camera and monitor system** — Watch your base from anywhere
- ✅ **Retinal scanner** — Biometric security
- ✅ **Laser tripwires** — Invisible intruder detection
- ✅ **Mines and traps** — Punish intruders
- ✅ **Full owner/whitelist system** — Decide who can do what
- ✅ **Reinforced blocks** — Blast-resistant construction

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: What is SecurityCraft?**
A: A comprehensive security mod that adds 50+ blocks and items for protecting your base — locks, keypads, lasers, cameras, mines, and reinforced blocks.

**Q: Is SecurityCraft free?**
A: Yes — 100% free.

**Q: What Minecraft versions are supported?**
A: **1.18 through 1.21.1**. Support varies by version.

**Q: Forge, NeoForge, or Fabric?**
A: All three are supported on respective versions.

**Q: How do I protect my base?**
A: Start with a **Universal Block Reinforcer** for your chests and walls. Add a **Keypad** to your door for a first layer of security.

**Q: Can other players break my reinforced blocks?**
A: Not by normal means. Reinforced blocks resist most tools and explosions.

**Q: How do cameras work?**
A: Place a **Security Camera** and link it to a **Camera Monitor**. You can view multiple cameras from one monitor.

**Q: What is the Codebreaker?**
A: A tool that lets you hack keypads — but it takes time and patience, giving the owner a chance to catch you.

**Q: What's the difference between mine types?**
A: **Standard** explodes on contact. **Bouncing Betty** jumps up before exploding. **Landmine** is a ground trap. **Naval** works underwater. **Trapped Chest** explodes on theft. **Reverse** triggers when disarmed wrong.

**Q: How do I disarm mines?**
A: Use **Wire Cutters**. Cut the wrong wire and it explodes.

**Q: Does it work on Bedrock / mobile?**
A: No — **Java Edition only**.

**Q: Can I use it with other mods?**
A: Yes — SecurityCraft is compatible with most mods.

**Q: How do I uninstall it?**
A: Delete the `.jar` from `.minecraft/mods/` and restart.
